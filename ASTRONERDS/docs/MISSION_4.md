# Mission 4: verify the Mars research data

Return toward base after saving S-01's scan onto the USB in Mission 3. Recover
the separate Mars Exploration chip from **(112,48)**. The signal is discovered within
12 units. Press **E** to collect it when alive, grounded, outside, within 1.5
units and with line of sight. The chip cannot be collected twice. Cliff rows 53
and 54 block movement except through x=114..118.

The task marker then points to the rescue ship airlock. Inside Map2 it points to
the Monitor Room PC at **(26.5,32.5)**. Press **E** within 1.8 units, grounded and
with line of sight, to upload the USB scan record and scan the chip. The PC
cannot open the quiz without the Mars chip. Both items remain in the inventory
until the ninth correct answer is confirmed, then are consumed immediately as used.

The terminal uses the four passages and nine questions in `Mission4.hpp`.
Read each new passage group before answering. Wrong answers stay on the same
question and allow unlimited retries. Rereading preserves progress. A correct
answer must be confirmed before continuing; all nine are required. The terminal
blocks gameplay, and pausing prevents answer submission and progression.

After the ninth correct answer is confirmed, the quiz closes, the task reads
**ALL QUESTIONS CORRECT**, and Mission 4 completes. Press **N** or choose
**Continue / Mission 6** to resume in the same ship and collect repair supplies.
The removed Mission 5 placeholder is skipped. Missions 6, 7 and 8 retain their
existing numbers and continue through transport, repair, charging and launch.
