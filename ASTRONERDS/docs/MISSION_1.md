# Mission 1 — Act 1

The campaign starts with supply recovery, defined in `include/horizon/Mission1.hpp`, includes supply delivery and material observations, then continues through carrier repair (Mission 2) and record copying (Mission 3). A new game resets supplies, samples, observations, delivery and copied records.

Mission 1 has no creature threat warnings, hunts or bite attacks. Normal battery use and shelter charging remain active.

## Task 1 — First-Aid Kit Recovery

Collect three green-outlined first-aid kits and two food/water supplement boxes. The five pickups are distributed between the material enclosures: two are in the open and three are enclosed separately by silicate minerals, water ice and rock fragments. The barriers are 2.1 world-height units tall, lower than their original design, but still too tall to jump over. Mine only the entrance you need with J or the left mouse button; destroying the entire enclosure is unnecessary. Click Collect near a pickup, or press E. Pickups never collect automatically.

Breaking the first barrier of each type gives exactly one matching sample. Further breaks of that type never increase the sample count, even while the first sample is being observed. Ordinary map resource deposits and crafting/rocket recipes remain disabled.

## Task 2 — Help the astronaut

After recovering all five supplies, approach the injured astronaut in the first Crew Room bunk on Map2 and click Deliver or press E. Three first-aid kits and two food/water boxes leave Inventory. After delivery, observe the three material samples to complete Mission 1 and start Mission 2: repair the carrier robot in the Cargo Hangar. Surface task markers lead to the airlock; indoor markers use the shared ship station coordinates.

## Task 3: material observations

The **Observed Table** is the three-sample desk in the ship's yellow-lit Storage Room. Approach the sliding door to open it automatically (it closes when Unit-01 moves away), then follow the green outline and rotating observation icon. Choose Silicate, Water Ice or Rock Fragments from the observation menu and deposit that sample. Only one sample can be observed at a time. The progress bar takes six simulation seconds; pausing freezes it. The sample returns to Inventory after inspection, with its total still limited to one. A separate green-outlined Craft Table beside it opens with C.

A material-specific information panel appears when inspection finishes. Close it before starting another sample. Silicate information explains silicon/oxygen-based minerals; water ice information explains solid H2O; rock-fragment information explains broken rock pieces and constituent minerals. Materials use distinct earthy, icy-blue and rough stone appearances.

The campaign ends after Mission 3's four-second record copy at the Monitor Room PC; press E nearby to begin. Mission 1 delivery and observations lead to Mission 2, where the scanned carrier needs a replacement wheel, robot arm and control chip. Its six-second repair unlocks the records task. See [Mission 3](MISSION_3.md). The nine-slot bottom hotbar displays a cookie/water supplement icon with the bag count and distinct silicate, ice and rock sample icons. Observed samples have a green indicator. Tab opens Inventory with sample counts and observation status.
